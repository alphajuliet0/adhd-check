## Original design choices

The domains, wording, fortnight recall window and response format are original design choices. The fortnight is a recall aid only. No total, cutoff, diagnostic probability, severity tier, norms or screening accuracy are claimed. The child pathway is an adult-observer form with an optional invitation to record the young person's words, not a validated self-report form. Lived-experience and clinician review, cognitive interviews and prospective independent validation would be required before screening claims.

No SDQ items or paraphrases are reproduced. Official SDQ and Vanderbilt downloads are optional external signposts. WURS is held out because permission is unresolved. No family-specific data is included.
